# Local short refill screen

The intended claim is that collecting several already confirmed free slots reduces total admission work and advances both application writes and durable drain for the frozen 128-job short queue. Use one buffered untimed baseline probe to choose the mechanism and freeze its only candidate before timing.

Run six five-arm blocks in three opposite-order pairs. Use the same rebuilt runner binary in every arm, the exact existing combined local broker, and duplicate writer-off and zero-hold controls. Compare refill against the two zero-hold controls in each group. A local result warrants a native request only if every group reconciles and the candidate beats both zero controls in at least five groups on both submit-to-terminal and enqueue-commit-to-terminal and on last entry, last first write and last second write. Each median paired gain versus the zero-control midpoint must exceed both median absolute duplicate spreads. Require at least 8 ms on the two terminal clocks and 4 ms on the three application milestones. The elapsed floor is about one fifth of the Linux enqueue-commit gap. Require the runner footprint and downstream stage deltas to stay within the predeclared tolerances or observed duplicate spread, as encoded in `campaign.py`. The native comparison would still be necessary to establish any Linux effect.

The untimed probe selected one candidate: wait for three free slots with a 500 µs absolute deadline from the first eligible sample. Of 34 successful refills, 16 requested one or two slots. Eleven of those saw at least three confirmed free slots by reply, six within 500 µs at the first sampled boundary. Median claim-session time was 1,251 µs, compared with 18 µs request-to-session and 16 µs session-to-dispatch. The candidate is opt-in for a single queue using split claims and the zero-hold success writer. Arm it only after a full confirmed batch. Immediate paths include explicit wakes, an empty or partial reply, a contention or error backoff, no other live handler, and an already met threshold. The refill deadline schedules reevaluation alongside the existing dispatcher deadlines. Existing reservation, SQL and launch fences remain authoritative.
